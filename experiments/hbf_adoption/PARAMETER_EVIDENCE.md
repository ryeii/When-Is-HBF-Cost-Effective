# What supports each simulation input?

The study combines released measurements, executable reference models, official metadata and declared scenarios. A literature citation does not turn a scenario into measured hardware. Exact revisions and byte hashes are in `resources.lock.json`, the native export manifests and each worker's source snapshot.

Upstream end-to-end accuracy is not inherited. Operator fit error, native-service agreement, route-traffic error and composite request-tail uncertainty are different quantities; the paper reports them separately.

Conventional policy coverage is also finite. The batch-cap-16, active-KV-near experiment does not implement FlexGen's multiple-microbatch weight-reuse schedule or CPU-resident active KV/attention. Offline results therefore do not establish superiority to optimized FlexGen. LLM in a flash's predicted neuron sparsity is not a property of our unmodified dense checkpoints and is not imported as a payload reduction. Adaptive expert replacement and cost-selected CPU/GPU splitting are further untested alternatives; free host acquisition and zero CPU arithmetic do not prove a universal conventional-performance upper bound.
